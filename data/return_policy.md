# TechNova Gadgets — Return & Refund Policy

## Standard Returns
Items purchased from TechNova Gadgets may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and includes all accessories.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout
- Gift cards
- Products with removed or damaged serial number stickers

## Refund Process
1. Start a return request from the "My Orders" page or contact support with your order number.
2. Ship the item back using the prepaid return label (provided free of charge within the US).
3. Once received and inspected (typically 3–5 business days), a refund is issued to the original payment method.
4. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after inspection.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different color or size of the same product are free within the 30-day window. Exchanges for a different product must be processed as a return followed by a new order.

## Late or Missing Refunds
If a refund hasn't appeared after 10 business days, first check with your bank or card issuer, then contact TechNova support at support@technova-example.com.
