# TechNova Gadgets — Shipping FAQ

## Shipping Options (US)
- **Standard Shipping** (3–5 business days): Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99
- **Expedited Shipping** (2 business days): $12.99
- **Overnight Shipping** (next business day): $24.99

Orders placed before 2 PM ET ship the same business day.

## International Shipping
TechNova ships to Canada, the UK, and the EU. International orders take 7–14 business days and may be subject to customs duties/taxes, which are the customer's responsibility.

## Order Tracking
A tracking link is emailed once the order ships. Tracking can also be viewed from the "My Orders" page.

## Delayed or Lost Packages
If a package shows no tracking movement for more than 5 business days, contact support so a trace can be opened with the carrier. Packages confirmed lost in transit are replaced or refunded at no cost.

## Address Changes
Shipping addresses can be changed only before an order enters the "Processing" state. Once an order ships, the address cannot be changed; contact the carrier directly to attempt a redirect.
