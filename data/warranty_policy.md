# TechNova Gadgets — Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
All TechNova Gadgets products come with a **1-year limited hardware warranty** from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## What's Covered
- Battery failing to hold a charge below 80% of original capacity within the warranty period
- Manufacturing defects (buttons, ports, screens, sensors)
- Water damage on products explicitly rated water-resistant (e.g., NovaBuds Pro, NovaCam Mini), when used within rated limits

## What's Not Covered
- Accidental damage (drops, cracks, liquid damage beyond rated limits)
- Normal wear and tear (scuffs, cosmetic scratches)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Loss or theft

## Extended Warranty
An optional **NovaCare+ Extended Warranty** can be purchased within 30 days of the original purchase, extending coverage to 2 years total and adding one incident of accidental damage protection.

## Filing a Warranty Claim
1. Contact TechNova support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. Support may request photos/videos or basic troubleshooting steps.
3. Approved claims are resolved via repair, replacement, or store credit, at TechNova's discretion.
4. Turnaround time for warranty repairs is typically 7–14 business days.
